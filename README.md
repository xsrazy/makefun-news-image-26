One image summing up the world news of Thursday 26 November 2026: a
newspaper front page drawn from scratch with PIL. Lead story: Trump
unveils new Section 301 tariffs on 14 trading partners, China capped at
20% and the EU at 15%. Supporting stories: at least 30 killed in a fuel
tanker fire on Cairo's ring road, Moscow's own figures putting Russia's
Ukraine war dead near 500,000, Bangladesh raising garment workers' pay
36% after weeks of protests, and Colombia marking ten years since its
historic peace accord. A ticker at the foot adds Thanksgiving in the US,
a record double century by a 15-year-old, North Korea expelling a Swiss
envoy, and Hera's close-ups of Dimorphos.

`main.py` draws the finished `entry.png` (1024x1024, under 1 MiB) with no
network access and no randomness: two runs write byte-identical files.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes `/output/entry.png` (`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` when that
variable is set). Requires Python 3 with Pillow installed.
